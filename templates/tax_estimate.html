Supported scope and limitations

Recorded cash income and approved expenses; configured partner allocation; regular SE method; standard deduction; ordinary capital assets; qualified dividends entered separately. No automatic legal expense decisions. No automatic QBI calculation, tax credits, itemized deductions, loss carryforwards, partnership basis/at-risk limitations, wash-sale detection, AMT, NIIT, foreign income, depreciation schedules or guaranteed payments. Full-year Utah residency assumed. Enter only accountant-reviewed QBI and adjustments; request review if your situation exceeds this scope. Recorded YTD inputs are not annualized.
